## 🎨 Color Scheme

### Primary Colors
- **Purple/Violet**: `#4c1d95`, `#782ca1`, `#9c35b0` - Main brand color
- **Blue**: `#2b68ac`, `#1fa4d8` - Trust & professionalism
- **Pink/Magenta**: `#ff34a3`, `#a1055a` - Erotic Forge accent
- **Green**: `#34ad81`, `#1b8232` - Success states

### Background Colors
- **Gradients**: `#f3ecfa` to `#e9e3f2` to `#fbeeff`
- **Light backgrounds**: `#f9f5fd`, `#f1f7fa`, `#f5f7fa`
- **White cards**: `#ffffff` with shadows

### Text Colors
- **Headers**: `#111827`, `#333`
- **Body**: `#473363`, `#555379`
- **Accents**: `#6e3793`, `#6b21a8`

## 📱 Responsive Design

The current implementation uses:
- Flexible layouts with percentage widths
- `maxWidth` constraints (650px, 740px, 920px)
- Margin auto centering
- Readable padding (24-54px)

## 🎭 Typography

- **Headers**: Segoe UI, 2.7em (Home), bold
- **Subheaders**: 1.25em, semi-bold
- **Body**: 1.18em, normal weight
- **Monospace**: For code/converted text output

## ⚡ Interactive Elements

### Buttons
- Rounded corners (6-14px radius)
- Padding: 10-34px
- Colors change based on context:
  - Primary actions: Purple/Blue
  - Erotic mode: Pink gradient
  - Success: Green
  - Disabled: Grayed out

### States
- Hover effects (implied by transition CSS)
- Disabled states for inappropriate actions
- Color-coded severity (Red=High, Orange=Medium)

## 🖼️ What Users See

1. **Landing**: Beautiful gradient page with two clear paths
2. **Work Area**: Clean writing space with smart tools
3. **Real-time Detection**: Instant feedback on content type
4. **Ethics Checks**: Clear warnings and approvals
5. **Transparency**: Complete audit trail of all actions

The design prioritizes:
- ✅ Clarity and ease of use
- ✅ Ethical transparency
- ✅ Professional appearance
- ✅ Respectful content handling
- ✅ Clear visual hierarchy
